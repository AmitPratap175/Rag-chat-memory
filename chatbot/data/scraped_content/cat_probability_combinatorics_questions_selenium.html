If x items out of n items are repeated, then the number of ways of arranging these n items is n!x!\dfrac{n!}{x!}x!n!​ ways. If x items, y items and z items are repeated within n items, they can be arranged in n!a!b!c!\frac{n!}{a!b!c!}a!b!c!n!​ ways.
